# Stock photographs — provenance and credits

Every photograph here may be redistributed. They fall into two groups, and the
difference matters to anyone who uses one in an exercise.

## Public domain and CC0 — nothing owed (20)

Use them however you like. No credit is required. This is the default for the
folder, because these files are copied onward by everyone who downloads a
scenario that uses one, and an obligation that travels that far lands on
facilitators who never agreed to it.

## CC BY and CC BY-SA — a credit is owed (7)

These are here because the subjects a cyber exercise most needs — a data hall, an
office floor, a production line — barely exist under CC0. Searching for them under
a free licence returns 1960s archive photography instead.

**If you show one of these to a room, credit the photographer and the licence**
wherever you would normally credit a slide. The Exercise Builder marks them in
amber in the image picker so you know before you choose.

Resizing to 1200px is a format change, which CC 4.0 permits without it counting
as an adaptation, so ShareAlike is not triggered by anything done here.

## Adding more

```
node tools/fetch-stock.js tools/stock-subjects.json ./candidates free
```

Downloads candidates for review rather than installing them, because the search
cannot tell a server room from a bicycle rack — both match "rack". Look at what
comes back, keep what is good, record it in `credits.json`, and run
`node tools/build-gallery.js`.

Pass `attributed` instead of `free` to include CC BY and CC BY-SA. Only do that
when nothing free will do, and always record the credit.
