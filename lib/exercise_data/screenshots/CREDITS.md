# Screenshots, documents and diagrams — provenance

The `.svg` artefacts in `screenshots/`, `documents/` and `diagrams/` are original
work, drawn for TTX Gym by `tools/build-artefacts.js`. **No licence attaches to
them and no credit is owed.** Use, change and redistribute them freely.

They are drawn rather than photographed because no stock library sells a ransom
note, a SIEM console or a fraudulent invoice, and because it means they stay
sharp on a projector, weigh a few kilobytes, and can be edited in a text editor —
the names inside them are plain SVG text, so an author can put their own systems
into one without redrawing it.

Names are deliberately generic, and placeholders are written as `[like this]`.
An exercise about your finance system is better served by a screen that says
FINANCE SYSTEM than by one carrying an invented company nobody recognises.

To change one, edit `tools/build-artefacts.js` and re-run it, or open the `.svg`
in a text editor and change the words.
